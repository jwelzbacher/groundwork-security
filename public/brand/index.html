01 — Idea

Lay the groundwork.

Groundwork is the missing layer for companies that need enterprise-grade proof without enterprise-grade headcount. The brand should feel like a foundation that is already load-bearing: warm, exact, Midwestern-honest. Not a SOC-in-a-box vendor. Not a hooded-hacker firm.

Earthy

Soil, clay, sand, canopy. The palette is dirt and fired earth. White and navy are off-brand.

Editorial

Fraunces headlines, short lines, kickers with a Clay rule. It should read like a well-set journal, not a SaaS dashboard.

Built

Stepped terraces. Courses of a foundation. We install programs that stay standing — the mark is that idea, compressed.

We are

Fractional CISO. Cloud groundwork. Certification path. Operator who still ships.

We are not

A lock icon. A fear pitch. “AI-powered” as a personality. Generic fintech blue.

02 — Mark

A foundation in 64 points.

The mark is a Soil tile with three stepped courses and a Sand keystone. It is an earthwork — not a shield and not a house. Do not redraw it.

  1. 01Tile Soil #1C2E26, 12px corners on a 64×64 square. This radius does not change.
  2. 02Clay course the ground line — #C4785A. Widest terrace, at the base.
  3. 03Sage course #A8B5A0. Steps in 4 units each side.
  4. 04Terrace course #5C7A62. The only place this green is required.
  5. 05Keystone Sand #F4EFE6. Caps the work. Keep the notch; it is not a letterform.

04 — Space & misuse

Give the courses room.

Clear space on every side is 25% of the mark’s width (16 units around a 64-unit tile). Minimum size: 24px on screen, 0.35 in in print. Below that, use the full-color tile only — drop the wordmark.

Do not

  • Recolor the terraces or swap Clay to the keystone
  • Drop a lock, shield, check, or cloud on the mark
  • Stretch, rotate, outline, or add a drop shadow
  • Place the open (tile-less) mark on photography
  • Set “Groundwork” in a second typeface
  • Use the Clay mono tile as the default avatar

Do

  • Use full-color on Paper, Sand, or white
  • Use full-color or Sand-mono on Soil
  • Keep the tile when the mark sits on a photo
  • Prefer the horizontal lockup in documents and decks

Out of bounds

Role, not just hex

Claude Design will see Clay and try to paint with it. Stop it. Clay is one accent per composition. Soil is identity, not a paragraph color on Paper (too close, too heavy). Sage is not body text on Paper — contrast fails. No second accent. No blue, no gold, no neon.

08 — Voice

Straight answers. Built things.

Write like Jonathan talks: an auditor who still ships, and who owns a restaurant, so he feels every dollar a program costs. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No theater.

Say

“Lay the groundwork. Get certified. Keep shipping.”

“A first 90 days that leave something standing.”

“Tell us what your buyers are asking for.”

“FedRAMP is a program, not a weekend.”

Don’t say

“Next-gen AI-powered cybersecurity platform.”

“We leverage synergies to delight stakeholders.”

“In today’s ever-evolving threat landscape…”

“Hackers are at the gate.”

Construction

install, baseline, path, standing, groundwork, course, handoff. Use them when they are true, not as decoration.

Honesty

Scope what you would buy for yourself. If they are not ready, say so. Prefer a scoped proposal to a generic deck.

Proof

Framework names are chips, not poetry: SOC 2, HITRUST, ISO 27001, FedRAMP, HIPAA. Credentials sit next to the work, not above it.

11 — Claude Design & Google Drive

One kit, two destinations.

Claude Design

At claude.com/product/design, create or remix the org design system. Upload this PDF, DESIGN.md, tokens.json, and mark.svg. Optionally paste the prompt in CLAUDE-DESIGN.md.

After extraction, generate a test landing page and an 8-slide deck. Reject output that is blue, uses Inter, or paints Clay as a full-bleed background.

Google Drive

Create a shared folder Groundwork Brand. Put the PDF at the root for anyone who needs to make a file. Put logos/svg in a Logos subfolder — Drive previews SVG; download for Figma/Illustrator/Canva.

New one-pagers and decks: start in Claude Design or Google Slides using Paper background, Fraunces titles, Clay buttons, then export PDF back into the same Drive folder.
