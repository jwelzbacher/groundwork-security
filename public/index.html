The problem

Enterprise buyers want proof. You need a program, not a binder.

Most growing companies hit the same wall: a customer sends a security questionnaire, a SOC 2 ask, or a HITRUST requirement — and there is nobody on staff whose job is to own it. Groundwork is the missing layer: we build the cloud and process foundation first, then run the certification path on top of it.

Questions

Straight answers before we get on a call.

Do you replace a full-time CISO?

For most small and midsize companies, yes — as a fractional seat. If you later hire in-house, Groundwork can hand off a living program instead of a pile of documents.

Can you set up and manage Vanta, Drata, or Secureframe for us?

Yes. We implement and administer compliance automation platforms including Vanta, Drata, Secureframe, Sprinto, Thoropass, and Hyperproof: integrations, policies, control mapping, failing-test remediation, evidence, vendor risk, and auditor access. We can run the platform for you month to month, or set it up and train your team to own it.

Which GRC platform should we choose?

It depends on your frameworks, cloud and HR stack, team size, and budget. We are independent of the vendors, so we compare Vanta, Drata, Secureframe, and others against your situation and recommend the one that fits — or tell you when a spreadsheet and a good auditor are enough for now.

Do you set up and manage third-party security tools too?

Yes. We deploy, configure, and operate the tools your controls depend on — MDM, EDR, SSO, SIEM, cloud security, vulnerability scanning, password management, and security awareness training — and connect them to your GRC platform so the evidence flows automatically.

Can you get us FedRAMP authorized?

We do FedRAMP readiness: boundary, controls, inheritance, documentation, and 3PAO prep. Authorization still depends on your agency path, 3PAO, and operating budget. We will tell you if you are not ready rather than sell a fantasy timeline.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Based in the St. Louis metro (Belleville, Illinois) and work with teams nationwide. On-site tabletops and workshops are available when they earn their travel.

How fast can we start?

Most readiness sprints begin within two weeks of a scoped proposal. Send the customer ask or questionnaire with your note and you will get a reply within one business day.
