Fractional CISO & GRC

A security leader on a monthly cadence: strategy, customer questionnaires, vendor risk, IR tabletops, and a KRI dashboard.

02Certification readiness

SOC 2, HITRUST, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and FedRAMP readiness: scoping, policies, control implementation, evidence automation, and auditor selection.

Your GRC platform (Secureframe, Vanta, etc.), managed: controls, evidence, risks, and vendor reviews kept current between audits. Plus internal audit co-sourcing.

Security engineering & automation

04Cloud configuration

We configure AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, or another provider to an audit-shaped baseline: identity, logging, encryption, backups, detection, and alerting.

05Security automation

Automations that keep controls running: evidence collection, IAM-change alarms, ticketed vulnerability remediation, and CI hooks.

06AI automations & integrations

Built on Claude, OpenAI, and Cursor: agentic workflows for your team, auditor copilots, control mapping, and questionnaire responses.
